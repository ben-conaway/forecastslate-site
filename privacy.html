Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

ForecastSlate is a weather application designed to provide weather forecasts, conditions, alerts, and related information on supported devices.

Information We Process

ForecastSlate may process location information that you provide to the application, such as a city, place, or geographic coordinates, in order to retrieve weather information for that location.

ForecastSlate is designed to operate without requiring you to create an account or provide your name, phone number, or other identifying information.

Weather Data Providers

ForecastSlate uses third-party weather data services to retrieve forecast and weather information. Information necessary to obtain weather data, which may include geographic coordinates, may be transmitted to those services.

ForecastSlate currently uses Open-Meteo for weather forecast data. Additional weather-alert or weather-data providers may be used depending on your location and the features available in the application.

Information We Do Not Sell

ForecastSlate does not sell your personal information.

Local Preferences

Certain application preferences, such as saved locations and display settings, may be stored locally on your device or through the platform's application storage facilities so that your preferences can be restored.

Third-Party Services

Weather information and related services may be provided by third parties. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Children's Privacy

ForecastSlate is not specifically directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
